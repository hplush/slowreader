# Likes and Feed Statistics

Posts of your feeds have like and dislike buttons in the feed reader and in the post popup.

Feed popup, feeds list, and the page of a feed show how many posts of the feed you saw during the last 90 days and how many of them you liked, opened, or disliked, compared with your most read feed. Feed popup also lists all liked and disliked posts of the feed.

Likes and statistics are synced between your devices with the same encryption as other data and are saved to the backup file.
