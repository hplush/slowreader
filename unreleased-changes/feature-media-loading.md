# Media Loading Reworked

Telegram photos and videos load in seconds instead of minutes. For this Telegram shows you online, while the app is on the screen. Premium accounts download more files at once. Images load from the place you look at, an image from the feed is not downloaded again in the post, and a failed one is tried once more. Feeds show a smaller copy of Telegram, Bluesky, and Mastodon photos until the full one is saved for offline. Saving for offline takes images before videos, skips posts read during the download, does not download Telegram media shown on the page again, and waits instead of failing with “Too many requests”.
